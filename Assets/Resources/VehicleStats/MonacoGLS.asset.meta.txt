Monaco GLS - Compact Luxury SUV

Class Name: SUV
Description: A compact luxury crossover combining urban agility with premium comfort. Higher ride height provides commanding road presence.

Performance Stats:
- Top Speed: 195 km/h
- Acceleration Time: 6.2 (0-100 feel)
- Handling: 0.85
- Drift Factor: 0.35 (stable, not drift-focused)
- Braking: 1.1 (good brakes)

Physics:
- Mass: 1850 kg
- Center of Mass Offset: (0, -0.25, 0.15)

Notes:
- Higher mass = more momentum, harder to stop
- Lower handling = wider turns
- Low drift factor = stable in corners
- Good for beginners who want stability over speed
